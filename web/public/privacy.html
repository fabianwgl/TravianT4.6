Privacy

This local distribution stores player names, email addresses, password hashes, messages, gameplay state, session data, IP records, and operator logs in its MariaDB, Redis, runtime, and backup volumes.

No payment, analytics, newsletter, Telegram, hosted help, or third-party email service is enabled.

Download your account data

Open Account options in the game and use Download account data. Confirm your current password to download a JSON file for this world, including your profile, messages, notes, and game data. Sitters cannot download the owner's data. Passwords and login tokens are excluded. The file describes its scope; operator logs, backups, and records from separate global services require an authenticated request to your operator. Keep the downloaded file private.

Export requests are limited to five attempts per 15 minutes and one download per minute. An incorrect password counts as an attempt.

Account deletion

Authenticated players can schedule gameplay account deletion in Account options. Full personal-data erasure and a completion deadline have not yet been verified. Deleting containers does not erase volumes or separate backups.

Do not expose this release publicly without operator contact details, exact retention periods, a jurisdiction-appropriate privacy policy, and independent review. See the repository privacy documentation for the full inventory.
